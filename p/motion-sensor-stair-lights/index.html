These are slim, rechargeable lights with a motion sensor. Stick them along the stairs or under a cabinet, and they glow warm white when someone walks past, then switch off by themselves. Handy when holiday guests are finding their way at night.

Why we like it

  • Turns on when it senses motion, then off again after about 15 seconds
  • Rechargeable battery, so no wiring

  • No plug or remote is included; you recharge them.

Who it's for

Anyone with dark stairs or hallways, and hosts expecting overnight guests.

Quick questions

How do they stick on?

They're magnetic. For non-metal surfaces, stick the included adhesive plate first, then attach the light.

How far does the sensor reach?

The listing says about 10 feet, at a 120-degree angle.

Do they need batteries?

No, each light has a built-in rechargeable battery.
