Quick questions

Do I need tools?

No. The base is self-adhesive and the strap loops around the garland.

How do I put them up?

Clean the spot, peel the backing, press hard, wait about ten seconds, then loop the strap around the garland and fasten it.

How many are in the pack?

24 brown silicone hangers, each about 7.9 inches long.
