About Found at Home

Found at Home is a small, faceless page about useful things for the kitchen, cleaning, organization and decor. We make short videos about one find at a time and keep every find in one list on this site, so you don't have to dig through captions to get the link.

How we pick products

  • We start from what people are already looking for: search interest, seasonal moments and products that solve a common home problem.
  • We read the product listing and only repeat what it says the product does. We don't invent features, numbers or results.
  • We don't show prices, ratings or "deal" claims. Those change all the time, so check them on Amazon before you buy.
  • Unless a video says so, we haven't tested the product ourselves, and we won't say we did.

Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. The "Check it on Amazon" buttons are affiliate links: if you buy through one, Amazon pays us a small commission at no extra cost to you. That money keeps the page running. It never decides what a note says.

Where we use AI

Some of our videos use an AI voice and AI-generated visuals. Generated scenes are illustrations of the problem or the idea, not footage of the real product, and we label them on each platform. Check the Amazon listing to see the actual product.

Privacy

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. When you click through to Amazon, Amazon's own privacy notice applies there.
